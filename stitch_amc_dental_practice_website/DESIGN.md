## Brand & Style

This design system sets the standard for high-end boutique dentistry, bridging state-of-the-art clinical expertise with warm, architectural hospitality. The brand targets discerning patients seeking restorative, cosmetic, and general dental care who value an unhurried, empathetic environment over cold commercialism. The emotional landscape is designed to dismantle dental anxiety: it evokes profound composure, quiet authority, tactile softness, and meticulous clinical precision.

The design movement synthesizes **Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth and Editorial Structure**. It departs from the abrasive, electric cyan tropes of mass-market dentistry in favor of rich deep navy structures, muted sage teal accents, warm sandstone foundations, and generous negative space. Interfaces behave like architectural gallery spaces: expansive, orderly, serene, and effortlessly navigable.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an organic, reassuring interplay between oceanic depth and tactile organic warmth:

- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Deep Navy):** The structural bedrock. Used for authoritative typography, high-priority actions, primary framing, and navigational clarity. Imparts confidence and clinical reliability.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A68` - Sage Teal / Muted Aqua):** A calm, restorative tone derived from apothecary botanicals and healing mineral baths. Applied to interactive highlights, confirmed statuses, and secondary interactive moments.
- **Tertiary (`#EAE3D9` - Sandstone Linen):** An organic intermediate neutral that infuses gentle warmth into structural surfaces, pill badges, and elevated card backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#FAF9F6` - Soft Ivory):** The primary canvas hue. Softened away from stark fluorescent white to eliminate eye strain and cultivate an inviting, private-clinic atmosphere.

### Functional & Semantic Roles
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for clinical input fields, active appointment cards, and foreground focal panels to retain immaculate cleanliness.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2DDD5`):** Ultra-delicate, architectural boundary lines providing soft containment without optical noise.
- **Text Muted (`#596573`):** Neutralized slate-navy for secondary annotations, procedural descriptions, and metadata.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels, marrying modern geometric rigor with humanist curves. This delivers clear, legible clinical instructions while retaining a welcoming, approachable bedside manner.

Headlines employ medium and semi-bold weights with negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to project architectural structure, sophistication, and editorial refinement. Body copy is calibrated at generous line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) to provide unhurried, comfortable readability for patient case notes, treatment plans, and post-operative guidance. Uppercase labels (`label-caps`) are paired with tracked letter-spacing (`0.1em`) for clinical taxonomy, category pills, and appointment milestones.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a fluid 12-column grid system designed around air, quietness, and rhythm. Generous outer margins isolate content zones, creating visual decompression corridors that reduce patient cognitive friction.

### Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 48px margins, 24px gutters. Clinical dashboards, practitioner profiles, and treatment overviews utilize asymmetric multi-column splits (e.g., 4:8 or 5:7 ratios) to achieve editorial elegance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 20px gutters. Secondary sidebars collapse into sticky bottom navigation rails or sheet drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px margins, 16px gutters. Treatment cards and booking steps stack vertically into clean, single-column sequences with fixed thumb-reachable primary actions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects sharp drop-shadows and synthetic glows in favor of **ambient diffused layering** combined with **subtle low-contrast borders**. This replicates the sensation of crisp, tactile paper stock layered upon natural stone counters.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Soft Ivory (`#FAF9F6`), flush, absorbing the underlying tone of the application.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Structural Panels):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Sandstone (`#F4EFEB`) bounded by a hairline 1px border of `#E2DDD5`. Shadow is minimal: `0 2px 10px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Modals, Flyouts):** Suspended surfaces with deep, whisper-soft diffusion: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.02)`, bounded by a soft slate-ivory border (`#DCD6CE`).
- **Surface Tiers:** Rather than dramatic z-axis separation, hierarchy is created through surface contrast (e.g., nesting a crisp white diagnostic result block within a sandstone consultation panel).

## Shapes

A soft, controlled corner radius (`roundedness: 1`) is applied throughout the UI. Base elements feature `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner geometry, creating tailored, architectural silhouettes that feel clinical, exact, and bespoke rather than childlike or overly bulbous. 

Larger containers, such as treatment modals and patient history dossiers, scale gently to `0.75rem` (12px). Pill-shaped exceptions are strictly reserved for status indicators, micro-badges, and discrete booking chips to communicate interactability and state at a glance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep navy background (`#0B192C`) with pure white text, 8px corner radius, padding `12px 24px`. Hover initiates a gentle transition to Sage Teal (`#2D6A68`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed by a 1px border of `#2D6A68` with Sage Teal text, shifting to a soft wash of `#2D6A68` (8% opacity) on interaction.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** Sandstone background (`#EAE3D9`) with Deep Navy text for low-pressure secondary confirmations.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Constructed on a crisp white base (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px slate-sandstone border (`#DCD6CE`) and an 8px radius. Text displays at `15px` in `#0B192C`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border tint to Sage Teal (`#2D6A68`) paired with an ambient teal focus ring (`rgba(45, 106, 104, 0.15)` at 3px spread).

### Cards & Dossiers
- Patient treatment pathways, procedure spotlights, and clinical notes are encased in crisp white panels backed by delicate `#E2DDD5` borders. Padding defaults to `24px` on desktop and `16px` on mobile.

### Chips & Badges
- Filter tags and procedural metadata utilize rounded pill geometries (`9999px`) with a Sandstone tint (`#F4EFEB`) and navy typography. 
- Clinical states (e.g., "Hygiene Check Complete", "Booking Confirmed") pair subtle tinted backgrounds (e.g., `#EBF3F2`) with crisp Sage Teal labels.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square-rounded (4px radius) checkboxes and circular radios utilizing Deep Navy check marks on an ivory fill, shifting to Sage Teal active states.

### Specialized Dental Components
- **Interactive Odontogram / Tooth Chart:** Minimalist vector representation featuring slate-line anatomical contours with hover states transitioning from muted ivory to translucent sage teal.
- **Before / After Comparison Slider:** Editorial split-frame viewer with hairline vertical dividers and minimalist circular handles for aesthetic and restorative portfolios.
